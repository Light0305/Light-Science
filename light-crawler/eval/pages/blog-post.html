Why I Switched to a Mechanical Keyboard for Fieldwork

I bought the Keychron K3 in April, mostly out of frustration with the mushy keyboard on my survey laptop.

Three months of fieldwork later, the battery still lasts about 34 hours between charges, which covers a full week of evening transcription sessions.

The low-profile switches reduced my wrist strain noticeably, because the board sits almost flat on the folding table I use in the survey tent.

It is not perfect: the keyboard weighs 396 grams, and the carrying case scratches far too easily for something sold as travel gear.

Even so, I no longer pack the laptop stand, because typing directly on the low board keeps my posture acceptable for two-hour sessions.
