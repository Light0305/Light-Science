Benchmark Results for Harvest Season Trial

We compared three mulching treatments across twelve field plots during the 2025 season. Each plot measured twenty by thirty metres and received identical irrigation.

TreatmentYield (t/ha)Soil moisture (%)
Straw mulch6.4231.8
Plastic film6.8734.1
Bare soil5.1324.6

Plastic film gave the highest yield in this trial, although the difference against straw mulch was within the margin the team pre-registered as practically equivalent.
