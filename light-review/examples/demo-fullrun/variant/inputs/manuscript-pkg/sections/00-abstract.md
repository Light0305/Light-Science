# Abstract

This study asks whether tracker thresholds for behaviour detection in recorded video can be predicted from video-level statistics instead of being tuned by hand for every new recording. Three arms are compared on held-out segments: a fixed-threshold arm, an oracle arm, and a predicted-threshold arm. The comparison is reported descriptively. The underlying analysis lines are untested or undetermined, so the manuscript attaches no confirmatory reading to the observed differences and states the limits of the comparison in the discussion.
