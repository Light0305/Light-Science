# Results

Segment-level scores were obtained for every arm on every held-out segment, and the distributions are summarised in the figure below.

[fig:F001]
Distribution of segment-level scores for the fixed-threshold arm, the oracle arm, and the predicted-threshold arm.

Relative to the fixed-threshold arm, the oracle arm differed by [num:N001] 5.52 points on the primary metric, and the predicted-threshold arm differed by [num:N002] 5.20 points on the same metric. The fixed-threshold arm, compared against its own repeated run, differed by [num:N003] 0.0 points, which records the repeatability of the pipeline under a frozen configuration. The spread of the fixed-threshold arm across segments was wide, and this spread is the main source of uncertainty in every comparison reported here.
